# What's New

What each version of Realistic Physics changed, newest first. The game shows its version on the start screen and in its bottom-left corner, and every Short made from the game shows the version it was made with. Only the newest version is online.

## v0.8 (2026-10-13)

- The game can be installed as an app with its own icon, and then opens from the home screen or the computer's apps without the browser's bars.
- The start screen shows how to install it in your browser, with an Install button where the browser allows one.

## v0.7 (2026-10-12)

- Skin, fat and muscle weigh and stretch as the real tissues do.
- Bone stays inside the hands and feet, and broken pieces of bone no longer fly out of a wound.
- The game shows its version on the start screen and in its bottom-left corner, and so do the Shorts made from it.

## v0.6 (2026-10-11)

- Injuries look as they really do, not exaggerated: blood comes only from wounds and is drawn at its real size, and a bruise reddens at once and darkens over a quarter of an hour.
- Crushed flesh shows as pulp instead of a hole through the body.
- Knife cuts open exactly where the blade went, and blows reach bone through the flesh over it.
- An about card tells what the simulation is, with links to how it was made.

## v0.5 (2026-10-09)

- Injuries follow published injury research: a firm sledgehammer swing can break the arm, and wounds bleed at real rates.
- Sound, made from what the simulation does.
- Knife cuts open into gaping wounds, fast blows split skin and muscle, and bones break where they bend most.
- Blood runs down the skin, drips off the body and pools on the floor.

## v0.4 (2026-10-06)

- A neutral mannequin body.
- Damage follows the speed of a blow: slow hits only bruise, while hard ones break bones and open wounds.
- Tools stay steady in the hand and move freely through wounds.
- The game starts with the sledgehammer in hand.

## v0.3 (2026-10-05)

- Plays in the browser.
- The sledgehammer, bat and knife have real shapes, and the knife cuts.
- The body takes the shape of a real human figure.

## v0.2 (2026-06-29)

- Rewritten in Rust, with a more human body shape, a rib cage, joints that can dislocate, bruised flesh, and cuts that spread along the skin.

## v0.1 (2026-06-20)

- The first version, written in C++: a body of skin, muscle and bone that blunt, sharp and heavy tools tear and break, with blood from the wounds.
