Realistic Physics

A 2D body-destruction sandbox where skin, muscle and bone stretch, tear, fracture and bleed through real simulation, not canned animations.

Content warning: graphic simulated injury, including blood, torn tissue and broken bones.

Drag
Swing the tool into the body; hold it there and drag on to press harder
B / S / H
Bat, knife or sledgehammer
Space / R
Pause / reset the body
Touch
Drag a finger to strike; tap the buttons along the bottom

Runs entirely on your device; nothing is uploaded. Source code · Demo video
